## What this fork changed

Renaming was not optional. Upstream's
[trademark terms](https://github.com/vorssaint/vorssaint-utils/blob/main/TRADEMARKS.md)
require any fork to use a different name, icon, bundle identifier, signing
identity and update feed — so the GPL covers the code, and the rebrand is what
makes redistributing it permitted.

- **Renamed** to Sundry throughout: app name, `com.sundry.utils` bundle
  identifier, preference domains, the privileged fan-control helper and the
  Now Playing adapter.
- **New brand.** New app icon, menu bar glyph and brand mark, generated by
  `Tools/MakeBrandMark.swift`. None of Vorssaint's artwork is used.
- **Upstream services removed.** Upstream shipped screenshot sharing, recording
  sharing and in-app feedback pointed at a server run by its maintainer.
  Continuing to use it would have sent this build's users' screenshots,
  recordings and diagnostics to a third party. Those endpoints are now unset:
  sharing and feedback report as unavailable until an operator configures an
  endpoint they run. See `ScreenshotSharingSupport.productionEndpoint`.
- **Upstream community and funding links removed.** The donation, Discord and
  social links pointed at accounts belonging to Vorssaint's maintainer. They
  are `nil` in `AppInfo`, and every button that opens one hides itself while
  unset, so no build sends anyone to an account this project does not control.
- **Added an Xcode project.** Upstream builds through `build.sh`;
  `Sundry.xcodeproj` produces the same bundle from Xcode. See
  [docs/XCODE.md](docs/XCODE.md).
